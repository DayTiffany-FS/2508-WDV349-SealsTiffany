## Sub-Topic 1 - Image Hosting
Basics of image hosting for websites. 

* Image hosting is a service that stores images on remote servers to be delivered to a website using direct links or embed codes.
* Sharing is viewed inside a platform and often can't be embedded externally.
* Using a CDN helps reduce latency.
* Possible platforms: Cloudinary, Imgur, Flickr, Unsplash, Google Photos, SmugMug

<br>

## Sub-Topic 2 - Adding Video to Website/App
Basic info about use of video on websites. 

* Text is best content for a site but video should be used when the moving visual is necessary and beneficial.
* Embed from trusted platforms like YouTube or Vimeo instead of direct uploads.
* Use official channels on trusted platforms, not an individual account.
