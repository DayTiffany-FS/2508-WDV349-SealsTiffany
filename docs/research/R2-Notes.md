## Sub-Topic 1 - Wireframing
Basic do's and don'ts of wireframing and it's importance to the process  

* Use to get a broad overview of the project  
* Not meant to be an extremely detailed view, this can come later  
* Keep it simple  
* Don't use color  
* Don't use too much detail  
* Focus on the basics  

<br>

## Sub-Topic 2 - Style Tile
Colorful views that demonstrate the brand   

* Good way to show the visual representation of what is trying to be   
* Show direct connection with actual elements that will be present  
* Listen to the client  
* Meet with the client to discuss, edit, and refine ideas  

<br>

## Sub-Topic 3 - Branding
Establishing a brand requires a lot of little details

* Multiple things are involved in brand identity (like font, colors, etc)  
* Know your brand personality  
* Style of font is directly reflective on the brand personality  
* Brand fonts should be flexible, have multiple font weights, and be legible  
* Know your budget and licensing requirements  
* Know your target audience and what colors reflect the message that's being conveyed
