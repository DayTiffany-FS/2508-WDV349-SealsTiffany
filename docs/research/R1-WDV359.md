## Topic - Starting a project

This document contains general notes related to starting a project.

<br>

## Sub-Topic 1 - Research Questions
A look at some of the tough questions that need to be asked in relation to making a project.

* What do you know about the project and the idea for the project?
* Do you know what has or has not been tried?
* What are the needs of this project?
* What are the negative impacts of this project?

<br>

## Sub-Topic 2 - Startup Ideas and Validity
A detailed look at what kinds of ideas make for a good startup project and why others are not. 

* Initial idea is a great place to start. It most likely will not be the end result.
* Solve a real problem, don't create a solution in search of a problem.
* Spend time evaluating the idea and don't wait for the perfect idea.
* How big is the market?
* Is the problem acute?
* Will there be competition?

<br>

## Sub-Topic 3 - Elevator Pitch
Basic description of an effective elevator pitch. 

* A short amount of time to sell yourself or an idea. It sparks interest in a longer conversation at a later time.
* Clear, compelling summary that shows professionalism, organization, and understanding the value of the work.
* Use clear language, avoid jargon.
* Should be persuasive so that the listener wants to care about your idea.
* Needs something memorable.
