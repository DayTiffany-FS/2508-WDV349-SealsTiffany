## Sub-Topic 3 - SEO Starter Guide
An SEO starter guide that details how to make your website easier to find by search engines.

* Google uses automated "crawlers" to discover pages on the web and adds them to its index.
* SEO helps these kinds of things understand content of the page and uses this to determine when it should be shown to users.
* Links and sitemaps help ensure your pages are connected so crawlers can find them.
* Having quality content that matches what is being searched for is necessary. This can be more easily done by using clear, relevant titles, headings, and descriptions.
* Titles and metadescriptions help summarize what the page is about to make it easier for search engines and users to understand it.
* URL's should also be kept clear, organized, and descriptive.
* Images should include a descriptive "alt text" so they can be seen and shown on the search engines.
* It is important to ensure that Google can fetch important resources like CSS and JavaScript that affect how the page looks and works.
* There are tools like Google Search Console that can help you see how your site performs.
* Use other means to promote your site, this helps make it more visible.

## Sub-Topic 4 - Google Analytics
A simple guide to what Google Analytics are and how to use them to improve traffic to your site.

* Google Analytics is a free web tool from Google that tracks and reports website traffic and user behavior. This allows you to understand who visits your site, how they interact with it, and whether your marketing tactics are working or not.
* It starts with a tracking code through JavaScript that is installed in your website pages.
* The tracker uses cookies and places them in the browser to collect data about a user's activity.
* This data is collected and recorded to show page views, clicks, the amount of time spent on the page, and any conversions that happen.
* The data is sent to Google's servers and processed into structured metrics and dimensions.
* Google displays the data in the Google Analytics dashboard in useful reports so that you can analyze the performance and trends of your users.
* These reports allow you to see which tactics are helping your site and which are not so you can make changes to improve your numbers.
